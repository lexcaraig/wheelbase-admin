# Wheelbase Community Guidelines

**Last Updated:** December 8, 2024

## Our Community

Wheelbase is a community for motorcycle riders to connect, share experiences, and ride together safely. These Community Guidelines help ensure Wheelbase remains a positive, safe, and welcoming space for all riders.

By using Wheelbase, you agree to follow these guidelines. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban.

---

## Prohibited Content & Behavior

### 1. Illegal Activities

**DO NOT:**
- Post, promote, or facilitate illegal activities
- Share content related to drug use or trafficking
- Coordinate illegal street racing or stunt riding on public roads
- Promote or sell stolen goods
- Share instructions for illegal modifications
- Encourage reckless or dangerous riding that violates traffic laws

**Consequence:** Immediate permanent ban + law enforcement notification

### 2. Hate Speech & Harassment

**DO NOT:**
- Post hate speech targeting race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin
- Harass, bully, or threaten other users
- Dox (share private information of) other users
- Impersonate others
- Engage in coordinated harassment campaigns
- Post content intended to humiliate or shame others

**Consequence:** Permanent ban (first offense for severe cases)

### 3. Violence & Self-Harm

**DO NOT:**
- Post graphic violence or gore
- Glorify or encourage self-harm or suicide
- Post real-world threats of violence
- Share content depicting animal cruelty
- Promote terrorism or extremism

**Consequence:** Immediate ban + law enforcement notification

### 4. Sexual Content

**DO NOT:**
- Post sexually explicit content
- Post nudity (exception: artistic motorcycle photography with incidental nudity)
- Engage in sexual harassment or unwanted advances
- Share sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance)
- Use Wheelbase for dating or sexual solicitation

**Consequence:** Immediate ban (permanent for CSAM + law enforcement)

### 5. Spam & Manipulation

**DO NOT:**
- Post spam or unsolicited advertisements
- Use automated bots or scripts
- Engage in vote manipulation or fake engagement
- Create multiple accounts to evade bans
- Manipulate marketplace listings or reviews
- Engage in phishing or scamming

**Consequence:** Account suspension (7-30 days) or permanent ban

### 6. Misinformation

**DO NOT:**
- Spread false safety information that could cause harm
- Post fake emergency alerts or crash reports
- Impersonate emergency services or officials
- Share manipulated media without disclosure
- Post fraudulent marketplace listings

**Consequence:** Warning → Suspension → Permanent ban

### 7. Intellectual Property Violation

**DO NOT:**
- Post copyrighted content without permission
- Sell counterfeit goods on marketplace
- Use others' trademarks without authorization
- Plagiarize others' content or routes

**Consequence:** Content removal + potential account suspension

## Acceptable Use

**What we encourage:**

✓ Share your riding experiences and routes
✓ Connect with fellow motorcycle enthusiasts
✓ Organize group rides and events
✓ Buy and sell legitimate motorcycle gear and parts
✓ Ask for and provide helpful riding advice
✓ Report safety hazards and road conditions
✓ Celebrate motorcycle culture and community
✓ Support new riders with encouragement and tips
✓ Respect different riding styles and preferences

---

## Marketplace Guidelines

**When buying or selling:**

- Only list legitimate items you own or have permission to sell
- Provide accurate descriptions and photos
- Honor agreed-upon prices and terms
- Meet in safe, public locations
- Use secure payment methods

**DO NOT sell:**
- Stolen goods
- Counterfeit items
- Illegal parts or modifications
- Weapons or dangerous materials
- Complete vehicles (link to licensed dealers instead)

Scams and fraud will result in immediate permanent ban and may be reported to law enforcement.

---

## Group Rides & Safety

**When organizing or joining group rides:**

- Follow all traffic laws and regulations
- Ride within your skill level
- Wear appropriate safety gear (helmet, jacket, gloves, boots)
- DO NOT encourage reckless riding or law-breaking
- DO NOT organize rides on private property without permission
- DO NOT pressure riders to exceed their comfort level
- DO use the emergency features if someone crashes
- DO share road hazard information with the group

⚠️ **Wheelbase is not responsible for accidents, injuries, or violations during group rides. Riders participate at their own risk.**

---

## Reporting Violations

**If you see content or behavior that violates these guidelines:**

1. Use the "Report" button on posts, comments, or profiles
2. Select the violation type
3. Provide context (optional but helpful)
4. Our moderation team will review within 24 hours

**For emergencies (immediate threats, CSAM, etc.):**
- Contact local law enforcement immediately
- Email: safety@ridewheelbase.app with "URGENT" in subject

False reports may result in account penalties. Report responsibly.

---

## Enforcement Actions

### Warning
First offense for minor violations. Content removed. Account remains active.

### Temporary Suspension (7-30 days)
Repeated violations or moderate offenses. Cannot access account during suspension.

### Permanent Ban
Severe violations (illegal activity, CSAM, hate speech, threats). Account deleted. Cannot create new accounts.

---

### ⚠️ Zero Tolerance

**Child exploitation (CSAM), credible threats of violence, and terrorism will result in immediate permanent ban and law enforcement notification.**

---

## Appeals Process

**If you believe your account was suspended or banned in error:**

1. Email: appeals@ridewheelbase.app
2. Include:
   - Your username
   - Date of action
   - Reason for appeal
   - Any relevant context
3. We will review within 7 business days
4. Decision is final after appeal review

**Note:** Bans for illegal activity, CSAM, or credible threats are not subject to appeal.
