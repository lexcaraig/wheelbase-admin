# Wheelbase Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** December 8, 2024

## 1. Introduction

Wheelbase ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us and information that is automatically collected when you use the App.

### Personal Information

- Name and email address
- Username and display name
- Profile photo and bio
- Phone number (for emergency contacts)
- Date of birth (age verification)
- Location data (when using ride tracking)
- Motorcycle details (make, model, year)

### Usage Information

- Ride history and statistics
- Posts, comments, and social interactions
- Marketplace activity and transactions
- Group memberships and join requests
- App usage patterns and preferences
- Device information (model, OS version)
- IP address and mobile network information
- Push notification preferences and tokens

### Location Information

We collect precise location data when you use ride tracking features. You can control location permissions through your device settings. Location data is used to:

- Track and record your rides
- Show nearby riders and routes
- Provide emergency assistance
- Display location-based content
- Show group rides in your area

### Communication Data

- Direct messages between users
- Group chat messages
- Marketplace inquiries
- Support requests and feedback
- Push notification delivery status

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

### Provide and Improve Services

- Operate and maintain the App
- Provide ride tracking and mapping features
- Enable social networking features
- Facilitate marketplace transactions
- Process group join requests
- Improve and personalize your experience
- Analyze usage patterns and trends

### Communications and Notifications

- Send push notifications about:
  - Likes and comments on your posts
  - New followers and mentions
  - Group join request responses
  - Ride invitations and updates
  - Marketplace messages and offers
  - Emergency alerts
- Send in-app notifications
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Send important service updates
- Provide promotional communications (with consent)

### Safety and Security

- Detect crashes and send emergency alerts
- Contact emergency contacts when needed
- Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
- Enforce our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service
- Moderate content and user behavior
- Investigate and address violations
- Ensure platform safety and security

## 4. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications. By using Wheelbase, you consent to receive:

- Engagement notifications (likes, comments, follows)
- Social notifications (mentions, replies)
- Group notifications (invitations, join requests, events)
- Marketplace notifications (messages, offers)
- Safety notifications (emergency alerts, crash detection)

You can manage notification preferences in Settings or disable them entirely through your device settings.

We store your FCM device tokens securely to deliver notifications. Tokens are marked inactive when you log out or uninstall the app.

## 5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

### With Other Users

- Public profile information (name, photo, bio)
- Ride routes you choose to share publicly
- Posts, comments, and social interactions
- Marketplace listings and messages
- Group memberships (for group members only)

### With Emergency Contacts

- Your location during emergencies
- Crash detection alerts
- SOS notifications and ride details

### With Service Providers

- Cloud storage providers (Supabase, Firebase)
- Analytics services (crash reporting, usage stats)
- Payment processors (for premium features)
- Push notification services (Firebase FCM)
- Content delivery networks

### For Legal and Safety Purposes

- To comply with legal obligations and court orders
- To protect our rights, property, and safety
- To protect the rights and safety of our users
- To prevent fraud, abuse, or harm
- In connection with legal proceedings
- To investigate Terms of Service violations
- To respond to law enforcement requests

## 6. Content Moderation and Monitoring

To maintain a safe and respectful community, we may:

- Review reported content and user behavior
- Use automated systems to detect prohibited content
- Monitor public posts, comments, and interactions
- Review marketplace listings for compliance
- Investigate Community Guidelines violations
- Take enforcement actions (warnings, suspensions, bans)

Private messages are encrypted and only accessed when:
- Reported by a user for violations
- Required by law enforcement
- Necessary to investigate serious violations

## 7. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

- Data encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Secure authentication mechanisms (JWT with RS256)
- Regular security assessments and audits
- Access controls and monitoring
- Row-Level Security (RLS) on database
- Secure credential storage (Keychain/Keystore)

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through Settings > Account > Delete Account.

Some information may be retained for legal or legitimate business purposes even after account deletion:

- Transaction records (7 years - legal requirement)
- Safety incident reports (indefinitely)
- Content moderation records (3 years)
- Violation and ban records (indefinitely)
- Aggregated, anonymized analytics data

Deleted account data is purged within 30 days, except as noted above.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your information:

### Access and Update

You can access and update your profile information at any time through the app settings.

### Delete Account

You can delete your account and associated data through Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will remove:
- Your profile and personal information
- Your posts, comments, and interactions
- Your ride history
- Your marketplace listings
- Your group memberships

### Location Permissions

You can enable or disable location access through your device settings. Note that ride tracking and nearby features require location access to function.

### Push Notifications

You can manage notification types in Settings > Notifications, or disable all notifications through your device settings (iOS Settings > Wheelbase > Notifications).

### Marketing Communications

You can opt out of promotional communications while still receiving essential service notifications (safety alerts, account security, terms updates).

### Data Portability

You can request a copy of your data by contacting privacy@ridewheelbase.app. We will provide your data in a machine-readable format within 30 days.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Wheelbase is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately at privacy@ridewheelbase.app and we will delete the information within 72 hours.

## 11. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with third-party services:

- Firebase (Google) - Authentication, push notifications, real-time data
- Supabase - Database and storage
- Google Maps - Mapping and navigation
- Payment processors - In-app purchases

These services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies before using our App.

## 12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including:

- Singapore (primary data center - ap-southeast-1)
- United States (Firebase services)
- European Union (data backup)

We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@ridewheelbase.app.

## 14. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

European Economic Area (EEA) residents have additional rights:

- Right to access your data
- Right to rectification (correction)
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@ridewheelbase.app.
